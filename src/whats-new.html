v1.0.2 Current Version

December 2025

A major upgrade to the core experience. We've redesigned the dashboard and added powerful new scouting tools.

  • Redesigned Dashboard New unified "Identity Card" featuring your club crest, Prestige Badge, and your player card at a glance.
  • Scout View Analyze opponent clubs with detailed scouting reports—squad depth, key threats, and performance stats.
  • Previous Meetings Track your rivalry history with any club you've faced before.
  • Search & Rankings A dedicated tab for finding clubs and viewing consolidated Squad Rankings—know who the MVP is.
  • Background Sync Your matches now update automatically in the background so you're always up to date.
  • Glossary Quick access to stat definitions from match details and rankings.
Fixes: Theme-consistent icons and improved region display.
