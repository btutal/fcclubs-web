FC26 service status

Current information about FC26 club data, live updates, and access to saved history.

Current incident · Live updates paused

FC26 live updates are paused

EA's FC26 Clubs API is returning incomplete data for some requests, while other requests are failing. We have paused live FC26 updates in app versions that support this control.

We have not confirmed whether this is a temporary issue or a permanent change to FC26 services. There is no confirmed recovery time.

First confirmed
September 10, 2026 at 20:48 UTC
Last updated
September 10, 2026 at 21:08 UTC
Affected
New club statistics, match downloads, and some club searches. On current supported releases, live search, Scout, and new club setup are paused too.
Still available
Matches and history already saved on your device, switching between saved clubs with history, and Restore Purchases.
Missing summaries
Some iOS club names, crests, or summary stats may be missing even when saved matches remain. We are investigating this separately; a missing summary does not by itself mean your match history was deleted.
What to do
Keep FC Clubs installed and keep your existing club setup. Reinstalling or removing a saved club will not repair the provider's responses and may put locally saved data at risk. Check this page for updates.
Older app versions
Some older releases can only pause club synchronization, and the oldest releases cannot receive a remote pause or notice. Live features may continue to fail on those versions.
Need help?
Report a bug with the app platform and a short description.
