Current information about FC26 club data, live updates, and access to saved history.

FC26 service transition

FC26 live updates are paused

EA’s shared Clubs API now returns FC27 data. FC26 live updates remain paused to keep that data separate from your saved FC26 history.

Your saved FC26 matches remain available. FC27 is available on the App Store and Google Play, with its own club setup and separate history. Android 27.0.1 is live; iOS 27.0.1 is submitted for review, with 27.0.0 available now.

First confirmed
September 10, 2026 at 20:48 UTC
Last updated
October 8, 2026 at 21:24 CEST
Affected
New club statistics, match downloads, and some club searches. On FC26 app versions, live search, Scout, and new club setup are paused too. This does not pause FC27 features in versions 27.0.0 and later on iOS or Android.
Still available
Matches and history already saved on your device, switching between saved clubs with history, and Restore Purchases.
Missing summaries
For users still on version 1.1.9, the app keeps saved club details visible when overall stats are unavailable and can recover missing details from matches already on your device. Official lifetime totals remain unavailable if they were not saved. Missing summary data does not by itself mean your match history was deleted.
What to do
Update to the latest version available through the App Store or Google Play. Your saved FC26 history stays separate while you set up FC27. On Android, you can open FC26 History during setup, even when club search is unavailable. Keep your current installation and saved data when updating.
Older app versions
Some older releases can only pause club synchronization, and the oldest releases cannot receive a remote pause or notice. Live features may continue to fail on those versions.
Need help?
Report a bug with the app platform and a short description.

Available on the App Store and Google Play

A new season with FC27

Android 27.0.1 is live. iOS 27.0.1 is submitted for review; 27.0.0 remains available on the App Store.

Version 27.0.0 introduced FC27 club and player stats for PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the updated 13 archetypes, including Disruptor.

Keep browsing saved FC26 clubs and matches separately from FC27. On iOS, open Settings → Saved Data → FC26 History. On Android, open FC26 History from Settings or during setup, even when club search is offline. Existing Pro Stats purchases carry over.

Update on the App Store or get it on Google Play to start your FC27 season.
