Current information about FC26 club data, live updates, and access to saved history.

FC26 service transition

FC26 live updates are paused

EA’s shared Clubs API now returns FC27 data. FC26 live updates remain paused to keep that data separate from your saved FC26 history.

Your saved FC26 matches remain available. FC27 is available in iOS 27.0.0, with its own club setup and history. Android remains on 1.1.9 while its FC27 update is in development.

First confirmed
September 10, 2026 at 20:48 UTC
Last updated
September 22, 2026 at 16:36 UTC
Affected
New club statistics, match downloads, and some club searches. On FC26 app versions, live search, Scout, and new club setup are paused too. This does not pause FC27 features in iOS 27.0.0.
Still available
Matches and history already saved on your device, switching between saved clubs with history, and Restore Purchases.
Missing summaries
For users still on version 1.1.9, the app keeps saved club details visible when overall stats are unavailable and can recover missing details from matches already on your device. Official lifetime totals remain unavailable if they were not saved. Missing summary data does not by itself mean your match history was deleted.
What to do
On iOS, update to 27.0.0 through the App Store. Your saved FC26 history is preserved separately while you set up FC27. On Android, keep your current installation and saved clubs until its FC27 update is available. Reinstalling will not restore FC26 live updates.
Older app versions
Some older releases can only pause club synchronization, and the oldest releases cannot receive a remote pause or notice. Live features may continue to fail on those versions.
Need help?
Report a bug with the app platform and a short description.

Available now on iOS · Version 27.0.0

A new season with FC27

iOS 27.0.0 brings FC27 club and player stats for PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the updated 13 archetypes, including Disruptor.

Keep browsing your saved FC26 clubs and matches in Settings → Saved Data → FC26 History. That history stays frozen and separate from FC27. Your existing Pro Stats and extra slots carry over.

Update on the App Store to start your FC27 season. Android remains on version 1.1.9; its FC27 update is in development.
