Active incident June 23, 2026 at 18:45 CEST

Provider-backed club updates are delayed for some users

The data provider is currently responding inconsistently for some club history requests. You may see club stats, matches, or sessions stuck on older data until the provider responses recover.

FC Clubs is keeping saved data visible and will continue to load features that have a healthy response. You do not need to reinstall the app or change your club setup.

What To Do

Short guidance for players and clubs.

If your stats look stale

Keep the app installed and check back later. A stuck club profile usually means the provider is returning old or failed responses, not that your local data is lost.

If another feature still works

Use it normally. Leaderboards, search, and Scout can keep working when a different provider resource is degraded.

If you need support

Email support@fcclubs.app with your club name, platform, region, and what screen looks stale.
