Before your next Clubs session

Which game platforms and phones are supported?

FC Clubs tracks FC27 Pro Clubs on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. The companion app runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26.1 or later, and on Android 13 or later.

How do I start tracking my club?

Search for your FC27 club by name, choose the right result and select your player from the squad. After playing, open and refresh the app to save available matches and review your sessions. No FC Clubs account is required.

What is included free, and what can I buy?

Start with one club slot, saved match history, automatic session recaps, standard squad and player stats, opponent scouting and share cards. Optional one-time purchases unlock extra club slots, up to three in total, and Pro Stats for one slot or all three. Pro Stats and extra club slots are separate purchases.

What does Pro Stats add?

Pro Stats adds Second Assists, Interceptions, Through Passes, Dribbles Completed and Beats (take-ons) in match and session views. These advanced metrics depend on the available match data and are not lifetime totals. It is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Can I still see my FC26 history?

Saved FC26 clubs, matches and sessions remain separate from your FC27 season when they are on your device or in a recoverable backup. Open FC26 History in Settings to browse them. FC26 live updates are paused; existing Pro Stats purchases carry over. Read the FC26 service update.

How often should I refresh, and how much history is available?

EA provides a limited window of recent matches. Open and refresh FC Clubs regularly to save the matches that are available. Background refresh is best effort. Matches that were never saved and are no longer available from EA cannot be recovered by the app.

How do I keep my saved history when changing phones?

Use the app's optional iCloud sync on iOS or Google Drive backup and restore on Android before changing devices. These features require your Apple or Google account. Restore depends on the data available in your sync or backup; iOS and Android histories do not transfer between platforms.
