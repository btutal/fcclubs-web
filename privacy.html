Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Privacy Commitment

FC Clubs App is designed to minimize data collection. Some optional features (analytics, crash reporting, and telemetry) only run if you opt in.

Scope

  • Website: This website may use Google Analytics through Google tag to understand aggregate site traffic and page usage.
  • iOS App: The FC Clubs iOS app may process and store data as described below.
  • Android App: The FC Clubs Android app may process and store data as described below, including optional Google Drive backup and Google Play Billing features.

Data Stored for App Functionality

  • On device: Your club data, preferences, and match history are stored locally on your device.
  • iCloud sync (Apple CloudKit): On iOS, if you are signed into iCloud, the app may sync your data across devices using Apple CloudKit. This sync is tied to your Apple ID and governed by Apple’s policies.
  • Google Drive backup: On Android, if you connect Google Drive, the app may back up preferences and raw app data to your Google Drive appDataFolder so you can restore after reinstalling or moving to a new device. Backup payloads are encrypted and compressed before upload. Google Drive backup is tied to your Google account and governed by Google’s policies.
  • EA servers: The app communicates directly with EA Sports servers to retrieve your club statistics.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

On Android, optional purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details. The app receives purchase and product status so it can unlock owned features, restore eligible purchases, and keep local entitlement state in sync. Google Play Billing remains the source of truth for Android purchase ownership.

Optional Analytics & Crash Reporting (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics (opt-in): Helps us understand app usage and improve features. You can control this via the “Share Analytics” setting in the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (opt-in): Helps us diagnose crashes and stability issues. You can control this via the “Crash Reporting” setting in the app.
  • Firebase Remote Config + Installations: Used for debug/support feature gating (for example, enabling debug tools for specific installations). This is separate from Analytics/Crashlytics consent.

Optional App Telemetry (Our Service on Cloudflare Workers + D1)

If you enable “Share Analytics”, the app may also send lightweight telemetry to our own API (hosted on Cloudflare Workers and stored in Cloudflare D1) to help us monitor reliability and understand aggregate usage.

This telemetry may include:

  • App identifiers: a randomly generated installation ID, game version, app version, and build number.
  • Saved slots snapshot: platform, club ID, and your in-game player name (if provided).
  • Match references: match IDs and optional metadata (such as match type, timestamp, and club IDs).
  • Health signals: sync success/failure and timing, plus a daily “active install” heartbeat.

We do not use this telemetry for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

Third-Party Service Providers

  • Apple (iCloud/CloudKit): Used for syncing iOS app data across devices (when iCloud is enabled).
  • Google Drive: Used for optional Android backup and restore (when connected).
  • Google Play Billing: Used for Android purchases and purchase restoration.
  • Google (Firebase): Used for optional analytics and crash reporting (when enabled).
  • Cloudflare (Workers + D1): Hosts our telemetry API and database used to store telemetry (when enabled).
  • Telegram (optional): We may send operational notifications to a private Telegram channel (e.g., counts and health signals). These notifications are for service monitoring.

Your Choices

  • You can enable/disable Share Analytics and Crash Reporting at any time in the app settings.
  • If you disable “Share Analytics”, the app stops sending Firebase Analytics events and stops sending telemetry to our backend.
  • On Android, Google Drive backup is optional. You can connect or disconnect Google Drive in the app’s cloud/backup settings.
  • On Android, you can restore eligible Google Play purchases from the app’s purchase or settings screens.

Data Retention

We retain analytics, crash reports, and telemetry for as long as needed to operate and improve the app. If you have a deletion request, please contact us.

Contact Us

If you have questions or requests, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
